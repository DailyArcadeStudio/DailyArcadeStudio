The Lead Camel

  • Sets the pace — hurry or ease, that is your control
  • Everything behind it reacts, late
  • Faster pace scores more. Faster pace also stretches the line

A Caravan Camel

  • Four followers, four gaps to hold
  • Too tight and they collide. Too wide and one is lost
  • The whistle hauls the whole line back in — on a cooldown

Quicksand

  • The heaviest drag in the game
  • Slows one camel, not the line
  • So the gap ahead of it opens while you watch

Cactus

  • The gentlest drag — a nuisance, not a crisis
  • The most common thing on the sand
  • Dangerous only when the gap is already bad

Scree

  • Middling drag, sitting in open sand
  • Easy to read, easy to forget
  • Hits the camel you were not watching

Dust Devil

  • Spawns behind the tail and closes
  • Drags the last camel specifically
  • You cannot outrun it — you have to absorb it
