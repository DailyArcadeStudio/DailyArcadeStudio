Steer and duck. Free in your browser.

The Gondola

  • Eleven metres long and it does not turn quickly
  • Steer left and right — that is the whole helm
  • The hull leans into every turn, whether you want it to or not

Matteo

  • Stands the whole way — he is the tallest thing on the boat
  • Ducking is a separate button, and you will need it
  • He does not stop rowing when things go wrong

The Ferro

  • The steel comb on the bow — pure decoration
  • It is also the first part of the boat to arrive anywhere
  • Every gondola has one. Nobody removes it

The Low Bridge

  • One clear span, marked by a single lamp
  • Steering into the gap is not enough — you must also duck
  • Two solid piers either side. Those are not negotiable

The Bricola

  • Three lashed mooring posts standing in open water
  • Steer around it. That is the entire interaction
  • Somebody owns it. Nobody has ever moved it

The Moored Barge

  • Loaded with crates and tied up for the night
  • Eats one side of the canal outright
  • Wider than a pole — you need to commit early
