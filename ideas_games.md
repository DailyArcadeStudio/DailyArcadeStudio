# Daily Arcade Studio — ゲームのお題

1行1テーマ。`— ` の前がゲーム名、後ろが中身。
上から未制作のものが選ばれる（`output/<slug>.built` で判定）。

**方針**: 海外向けチャンネルなので**舞台を世界中に散らす**。
日本モチーフは全体の1〜2割まで。半分は場所を問わない抽象的な題材にする。
操作が1〜2種類で、説明が要らないものを優先する。

## 世界各地

- Ninja Dash — 3D endless runner, dodge shuriken on a torii road
- Lantern Drop — stack falling paper lanterns without toppling the pile
- Gondola Squeeze — steer a Venice gondola through narrowing canals
- Camel Queue — keep a camel caravan spaced across shifting desert dunes
- Tram Brake — stop a Lisbon tram exactly at each steep hillside stop
- Elevator Overload — let riders on and off without exceeding the weight
- Reindeer Gap — leap a sled over cracking Arctic ice
- Piñata Swing — time a blindfolded swing at a moving piñata
- Sauna Ladle — pour water to hold a Finnish sauna at the perfect heat
- Tuk Tuk Weave — thread a Bangkok tuk-tuk through gridlocked traffic
- Windmill Sails — furl Dutch windmill sails before the gale tears them
- Bagpipe Breath — keep the bag inflated while the tune speeds up
- Llama Stack — load a llama without unbalancing the load
- Bull Run Alley — sidestep down a Pamplona street, one gap at a time
- Kilimanjaro Steps — climb while thin air shortens every stride

## 場所を問わない

- Fuse Box — reroute power before each breaker pops
- Bridge Bolt — bolt a span as traffic crosses it behind you
- Pendulum Park — drop a wrecking ball on marked blocks only
- Balloon Ascent — vent gas to hold a balloon in a narrow altitude band
- Domino Chain — place dominoes so the chain reaches the far switch
- Magnet Sort — flip polarity to steer falling metal into the right bin
- Gravity Well — slingshot a probe past drifting planets to the exit
- Ice Cube Slide — melt and refreeze a cube to fit each gap
- Conveyor Panic — sort parcels as the belt keeps accelerating
- Spotlight Escape — cross a stage while sweeping spotlights hunt you
- Cable Car Balance — shift passengers to keep the car level
- Sandcastle Tide — build above a rising tide line
- Mirror Maze — angle mirrors to land a beam on the target

## 操作の型を散らす（既存に無いジャンルを優先）

既存は「避ける」「速度を保つ」に偏っている。以下は操作の性質が違うものを
意識的に集めたもの。似た絵面が続かないよう、上から順に消化する。

### 記憶・観察
- Night Watch — memorise which windows were lit, then spot the one that changed
- Market Swap — two stalls swap goods behind a cart; track where the fish went
- Lighthouse Code — repeat back the flash pattern a ship just signalled

### 狙う・当てる
- Coconut Shy — lob at a coconut while the wind shifts each throw
- Curling Sweep — sweep the ice to steer a stone into the house
- Fountain Coin — bounce a coin off the rim into a Trevi fountain basin

### 組み立てる・つなぐ
- Pipe Panic — rotate pipes to route water before the tank overflows
- Kite String — untangle two kite lines without letting either touch ground
- Loom Weave — pass the shuttle in the right order or the pattern breaks

### 選ぶ・見分ける
- Customs Line — wave through travellers, stop the one with contraband
- Mushroom Basket — pick only the edible ones as the light fades
- Coffee Order — pull the right shot before the queue loses patience

### 押し引き・力加減
- Tug of War — time the pull against a rope that slackens and snaps taut
- Wine Cork — ease the cork out without snapping it
- Bell Tower — swing the rope so the bell rings on the hour, not before

### 守る・配置する
- Sandbag Line — stack bags where the river will breach, not where it looks worst
- Beehive Smoke — puff smoke to calm the swarm before it reaches the hive door
- Fire Break — clear the right trees so the fire runs out of fuel
