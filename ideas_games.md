# Daily Arcade Studio — ゲームのお題

1行1テーマ。`ledger.py` が上から未制作のものを選ぶ。
テーマは「操作が1〜2種類で説明が要らないもの」を優先する。

- Ninja Dash — 3D endless runner, dodge shuriken on a torii road
- Lantern Drop — stack falling paper lanterns without toppling the pile
- Koi Current — steer a koi upstream through narrowing rapids
- Roof Runner — leap across Edo rooftops, tiles crumble behind you
- Bamboo Slice — cut falling bamboo at the marked joint, timing only
- Tea Pour — fill cups to the line, overfill and you lose the round
- Kite Line — keep a kite aloft through shifting wind columns
- Fox Mask — match the mask that flashes, one wrong pick ends it
- Stone Garden — rake sand into the pattern before the bell
- Firefly Jar — catch fireflies without letting the caught ones escape
- Sumo Push — time the shove, get pushed out and you lose
- Umbrella Rain — angle an umbrella to deflect falling objects
- Noodle Pull — stretch dough to the target length, snap and you lose
- Shrine Steps — climb steps while the crowd pushes back down
- Crane Fold — fold along the highlighted crease, speed increases
- Night Ferry — dock a boat against a drifting current
- Drum Match — repeat the taiko pattern, it gets one beat longer each time
- Cat Alley — cross a rooftop alley without waking the sleeping cats
- Ink Brush — trace the kanji stroke before the ink dries
- Snow Roof — sweep snow off a roof before the weight collapses it
